Smart Marking — Teacher flow

Round 1 of 3 (teacher → student → components). Desktop artboards at 1440, review queue and page sorter at 1024. Key states only. Pan and zoom to inspect.

Assumptions

Classlists are ~40 students; tables show the first rows and a count.

Register numbers are integers 1–45; class codes are 4 chars from the unambiguous alphabet.

"Needs you" is the only thing a teacher must act on; everything else is informational.

Settings tab is deliberately thin (rename, archive, regenerate code) and shown as a row, not an artboard.

Colour roles

Red primary action, the single accent. One per screen.

Amber "Needs you" only. Derived in OKLCH to sit on the Modernist ramp scale; paired with a label and icon, never colour alone.

Ink Released / Ready — finished states are solid ink, so red stays for actions.

Grey Draft, Marking, Submitted — in-progress, nothing to do.

Design reasoning

Modernist grid: 2 px rules separate every major band; content is flush left, no rounded corners.

The review queue is where time goes, so it is a two-column reading layout: evidence on the left, decision on the right, one hand on the keyboard.

The page sorter treats a scanned booklet as a strip of pages; a student row is a drop target and an ordered strip at once.

Copy uses school vocabulary: hand in, register number, classlist, release.
