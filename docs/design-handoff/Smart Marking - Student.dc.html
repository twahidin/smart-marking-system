Smart Marking — Student flow

Round 2 of 3. Phone artboards at 375 × 812. Same Modernist tokens and colour roles as the teacher flow; body text never below 16 px, every control ≥ 44 px. See the teacher flow for round 1.

Assumptions

The student arrives from the class link, so CE4R- is already known; they only type their number.

No account. Identity is shown on every screen with "Not you?" so a wrong number is caught early.

Teacher-facing words (escalation, confidence, reviewer, rubric) never appear here.

Students also open the link on school iPads and Chromebooks. The phone layout is the base; above 768 px it becomes two columns (S10, S11). Same components, no separate app.

Colour roles

Red the one thing to do next: Continue, Hand in.

Amber something to check: a blurry page, a lost connection, a wrong number. Always with words and an icon.

Ink Feedback ready — finished, solid, unmissable in a list.

Grey Handed in, marking — nothing to do.

Design reasoning

Marks are shown as a number and a row of squares (■■■□□), never colour alone.

Feedback is one long reading page in the order the brief gives: total, summary, strengths, question by question, work on next, next steps. Each question opens in place to show criteria and the student's own page.

Waiting states are calm: a timestamp and a sentence, no spinner.
